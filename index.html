💛
मैत्री
Mettā
Loving-Kindness

Cultivating an unconditional wish for each other's wellness, safety, and happiness through every season of life.

🕊️
करुणा
Karuṇā
Compassion

Holding one another tenderly during trials, offering a compassionate ear and gentle solace without judgment.

🌸
मुदिता
Muditā
Sympathetic Joy

Celebrating each other's triumphs, creative milestones, and inner growth with pure, unselfish gladness.

⚖️
उपेक्षा
Upekkhā
Equanimity

Remaining anchored in balance, serenity, and mutual trust amid the ever-changing tides of life.
